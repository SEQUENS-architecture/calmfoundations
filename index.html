Services

What I offer

Every session is planned around your child. Mix and match: many families combine tuition with wellbeing support.

One-to-one tuition

Ages 4 to 11 · in person or online

Patient, structured teaching that fills gaps and builds confidence.

  • Reading, writing, phonics and spelling
  • Maths: number, times tables, problem solving
  • Preparing for tests and moving up to secondary

Social and emotional learning and PSHE

Individual or small groups

Helping children understand their feelings and get along with others.

  • Naming and managing big feelings
  • Friendships, confidence and self-esteem
  • Worry, change and transitions

Therapeutic art

Ages 4 to 12 · individual sessions

A calm, creative space where children can express what is hard to put into words.

  • Painting, drawing, clay and collage
  • Building calm and self-regulation
  • No art skills needed

School consultancy

For leaders, teachers and support staff

Practical support that works in real classrooms.

  • Inclusion, SEN and adaptive teaching
  • Behaviour and wellbeing approaches
  • PSHE planning, staff training and coaching

Parent advice: SEN and behaviour

One-off consultations or regular check-ins

Clear, kind, practical advice for home and for working with your child’s school.

  • Understanding your child’s needs and what helps
  • Routines, meltdowns, boundaries and calm-down plans
  • Preparing for school meetings and support plans

Questions

Frequently asked

Where do sessions take place?

In your home, at a quiet local venue, or online. School consultancy takes place on site or online.

How long is a session?

Tuition and wellbeing sessions are usually 45 to 60 minutes. Parent consultations are 60 minutes.

Does my child need a diagnosis?

No. I support children with and without a diagnosis, and can help you think about next steps if you have concerns.

Will you talk to my child’s school?

Yes, with your permission. Working together helps children get consistent support.
